title=Map Teleport – Vehicle & Trailer Add-on
description=WORLD-MAP TELEPORT
description=Adds independent right-click teleport actions to the B42.21 world map in single-player and multiplayer while keeping map, navigation/GPS, route, destination, marker, teleport and cheat-menu actions from other mods separately selectable.
description=
description=TELEPORT HERE: Also available while seated: teleports your character without the vehicle. Carried animals travel with you; rope-led animals can travel when you are on foot.
description=TELEPORT VEHICLE HERE: Moves the character, occupied vehicle and complete towing chain, including towed vehicles, trailers, animal trailers and their animals.
description=
description=Stop the convoy first. Players and other passengers arrive on foot; the empty vehicle and connected trailers are placed on open ground nearby, including roads, parking areas, grass or dirt.
description=The same transfer flow is used for nearby and distant destinations. If loading or placement fails, the transfer attempts to return everyone to their starting positions and seats.
description=
description=VEHICLE MARKER: In single-player, vehicle transfers add a temporary steering-wheel marker to the world map to help locate the landing position. It is replaced on the next vehicle transfer and is not a live tracker.
description=
description=Supports vanilla vehicles and mods using the game's native vehicle, towing and animal systems, including KI5, Autotsar Trailers B42, W900 Semi-Truck B42, Carry More Animal and others. Independent towing, animal transport or flight systems can receive mod-specific compatibility.
description=
description=BACKUP KEY: Bind UMTSP: Teleport Here (World Map) under Options > Key Bindings > Universal Map Teleport. On foot it teleports the character; while seated it opens a choice between character and vehicle teleport. Unbound by default.
description=
description=REQUIREMENT: Requires Map Teleport – Right-Click Rescue and ZombieBuddy 2.3.4+; add both as Steam required items so subscribers are prompted automatically. Multiplayer versions must match.
description=
description=【地图右键传送】
description=为 B42.21 单人及多人世界地图加入独立右键传送，并与地图、导航/GPS、路线、目的地、标记、传送及作弊菜单等模组选项同时显示、分别选择。
description=
description=【Teleport Here】坐车时也可选择只传人物，车辆留在原地；携带动物随行，步行时也可带走绳索牵引动物。
description=【Teleport Vehicle Here】传送人物、当前车辆及完整牵引车队，包括被牵引车辆、拖车、动物拖车和其中的动物。
description=
description=传送前车队须停稳。人物和其他乘客在目的地下车；空车与连接的拖车停放在附近空地，包括道路、停车场及无障碍草地、泥地。
description=近距离与远距离使用同一流程；加载或落点失败时会尝试让所有人返回起点和原座位。
description=
description=【车辆临时标记】单人传车时，世界地图会添加方向盘标记，方便寻找车辆落点；下次传车时替换，不会实时跟踪车辆。
description=
description=支持原版及使用游戏原生车辆、牵引和动物机制的模组，包括 KI5、Autotsar Trailers B42、W900 Semi-Truck B42、Carry More Animal 等。独立牵引、动物载运或飞行系统可按具体模组追加兼容。
description=
description=【备用键】在 Options > Key Bindings > Universal Map Teleport 设置 UMTSP: Teleport Here (World Map)。步行时直接传人，坐车时弹出人物／车辆传送选项；默认未绑定。
description=
description=【依赖】需要 Map Teleport – Right-Click Rescue 与 ZombieBuddy 2.3.4+；将两者关联为 Steam 必需物品后，订阅时会自动提示。多人双方版本须一致。
