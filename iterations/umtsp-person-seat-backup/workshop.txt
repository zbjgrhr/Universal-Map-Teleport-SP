description=WORLD-MAP RIGHT-CLICK TELEPORT: Adds an independent Teleport Here action to the Build 42.21 world-map menu in single-player and multiplayer.
description=MENU COEXISTENCE: Map, navigation/GPS, route, destination, marker, teleport and cheat-menu actions from other mods can remain visible and separately selectable.
description=TELEPORT HERE: Moves the character, carried animals and rope-led animals. Vehicles stay at their original location. All multiplayer players can use it without admin approval.
description=BACKUP KEY: Under Options > Key Bindings > Universal Map Teleport, bind UMTSP: Teleport Here (World Map). Open the world map, point at a destination and press the keyboard key. Unbound by default.
description=LANDING: The destination is checked for usable ground. Roads, parking areas and unobstructed grass or dirt can be used. If a distant landing cannot be validated, the character and animals attempt to return to the starting point.
description=NO VEHICLE DEPENDENCY: This edition does not require ZombieBuddy.
