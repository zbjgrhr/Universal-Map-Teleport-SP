# Map Teleport – Vehicle & Trailer Add-on

## 中文

为 B42.21 单人及多人世界地图加入独立右键传送，并与地图、导航/GPS、路线、目的地、标记、传送及作弊菜单等模组选项同时显示、分别选择。

**Teleport Here**：传送人物、携带动物和绳索牵引动物，车辆留在原地。

**Teleport Vehicle Here**：传送人物、当前车辆及完整牵引车队，包括被牵引车辆、拖车、动物拖车和其中的动物。

传送前车队须停稳。所有乘客保持座位并随所在车辆一起传送。车辆会落在目的地附近的道路、停车场或无障碍草地、泥地。

支持原版及使用游戏原生车辆、牵引和动物机制的模组，包括 KI5、Autotsar Trailers B42、W900 Semi-Truck B42、Carry More Animal 等。独立牵引、动物载运或飞行系统可按具体模组追加兼容。

**备用键**：在 **Options → Key Bindings → Universal Map Teleport** 设置 `UMTSP: Teleport Here (World Map)`。步行时直接传人，坐车时弹出两种选择；默认未绑定。

**依赖**：车辆传送需要 ZombieBuddy 2.3.4+；订阅车辆版时 Steam 会提示一并订阅。多人双方版本须一致。

## English

Adds independent right-click teleport actions to the B42.21 world map in single-player and multiplayer while keeping map, navigation/GPS, route, destination, marker, teleport and cheat-menu actions from other mods separately selectable.

**Teleport Here** moves the character, carried animals and rope-led animals. The vehicle stays behind.

**Teleport Vehicle Here** moves the character, occupied vehicle and complete towing chain, including towed vehicles, trailers, animal trailers and their animals.

The convoy must be stopped. All passengers remain seated and travel with their vehicle. Vehicles land on nearby roads, parking areas or unobstructed grass or dirt.

Supports vanilla vehicles and mods using the game's native vehicle, towing and animal systems, including KI5, Autotsar Trailers B42, W900 Semi-Truck B42, Carry More Animal and others. Independent towing, animal transport or flight systems can receive mod-specific compatibility.

**Backup key:** Bind `UMTSP: Teleport Here (World Map)` under **Options → Key Bindings → Universal Map Teleport**. On foot it teleports the character; while seated it opens both choices. Unbound by default.

**Requirement:** Vehicle transfer requires ZombieBuddy 2.3.4+; Steam prompts players to subscribe with this mod. Multiplayer versions must match.
